This page explains what data is collected when you visit illuminismo.net (“ILLUMINISMO”, “we”). The site is hosted on Google’s Blogger platform and operated by Nika Khutsurauli, the Netherlands.

What is collected

  • Server and usage data. Blogger (Google) records standard technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages visited, to run and secure the service and to produce visitor statistics.
  • Comments and contact form. If you leave a comment or use the contact form, the name, email address and message you enter are stored by Blogger and shown to us.
  • Language preference. The site remembers your chosen language (KA/EN) in your own browser’s local storage. It is not sent to us.

Cookies and advertising

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors’ cookies at aboutads.info.

When ads are active, visitors from the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised advertising cookies are used, and can change their choice at any time.

How Google uses data from sites that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Embedded content

Pages may embed or link to YouTube videos and to our galleries on illuminismo.github.io. Those services may collect data under their own policies.

Your rights (GDPR)

You may ask to access, correct or delete personal data you gave us (for example a comment or a message). Contact us through the contact page. You also have the right to complain to a supervisory authority; in the Netherlands this is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Changes

We will update this page when our practices change and show the date above.
