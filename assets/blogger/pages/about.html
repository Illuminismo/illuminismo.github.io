ILLUMINISMO is an archive of myth, memory and future. Ancient pantheons, epics, symbols and civilisations take a new form here — as tarot decks, illustrated epics, films, music and research articles.

What we do

Every piece should leave you with one of three things: a better question, a deeper context, or an image that stays with you. We write with sources: where we say “the text says”, the text and its passage are cited; where it is interpretation, we say so.

Five directions

  • Myth & Pantheon — gods, heroes and their stories, side by side.
  • Civilizations & Memory — cities, falls and what remained.
  • Symbol & Consciousness — religion, ritual, symbol, archetype.
  • Future & Society — what old knowledge means today.
  • Original Works — tarot, epics, films and music.

What ILLUMINISMO is not

Not a conspiracy brand, not an “ancient secrets” channel, and not an academic journal pretending to be neutral. Illustrations are made with the help of AI, and we always say so.
